Every Sherlock data point is tagged as simulated, benchmarked, or fielded. Simulated insights illustrate the structure of a module before you buy. Google Trends tells you what people search. It doesn't tell you why customers switch, what they'd pay 20% more for, or which growth move has the highest odds in your market. Generic industry reports describe the past. Sherlock reports drive your next decision.
